Lauterbrunnen day — or lake day, or gorge-and-caves

Grindelwald · last full day · four versions, and the group can split

Version 1 — Lauterbrunnen (Scott's outline)

  • getting there

    Two trains from Grindelwald, both with one change. Fast: via Zweilütschinen, ~50 min, every 30 min. Scenic: up over Kleine Scheidegg and down through Wengen, ~2 h, the best rail views in the region. Go scenic one way if the morning is clear.

  • early

    Start early. Lauterbrunnen village is small — a few cafés and a pub — and everything below is within a short walk, bus, or cable car. Plan to get there and then pick from the menu; nobody has to do the same thing.

  • easy

    Staubbach Falls (10-min walk from the station, free) and the flat valley path toward Stechelberg past 72 waterfalls, 1–2 h. Trümmelbach Falls — glacier water carving through the rock, bus 141 from the station, ~1 h inside.

  • up

    Cable car to Grütschalp, then the little panorama train (or a 1.5 h walk) along the cliff to Mürren, car-free, facing the three peaks. From Mürren the Schilthorn cable car goes to 9,700 ft and the revolving restaurant from the Bond film — pricey, worth it on a clear day.

  • hike

    Lobhornhütte (Sarah's find): bus to Isenfluh, cable car to Sulwald, 1.5 h up to the hut, where the warden cooks for you. Cash. Back down the same way or on to Grütschalp.

  • evening

    Regroup on the train home or at dinner in Grindelwald — the last night out. Pack; checkout is 10 am.

Other versions: 2 · Oeschinensee — train to Kandersteg, gondola, the ledge loop above the lake (best view in the Oberland, full day). 3 · Pfingstegg + Gletscherschlucht — both in the village, half a day. 4 · St. Beatus Caves + Harder Kulm — Lake Thun caves, then sunset over Interlaken. Two groups can go different ways and share the same train back for dinner.
Hardergrat, from the list: not the full ridge (see the Ideas page). Lombachalp → Augstmatthorn and back, 4–5 h, is the version to do if the group wants a big morning.
